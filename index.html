How the numbers are calculated

Left = left offset − left extent.
Right = room width − left offset − table width − right extent.
Top = top offset − top extent.
Bottom = room depth − top offset − table depth − bottom extent.

A negative result means this rectangular envelope crosses that boundary. A positive result is the distance remaining in this model. Assess furniture fit, movement and safe passage separately.
